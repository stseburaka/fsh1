About us

FSH1: Belarusian photography platform, whose main goal is to create a space and the conditions for free creativity and for connecting Belarusian photographers.

We organize meetings, discussions, and educational events, such as lectures, workshops, mentorship programs, and artist talks.

We build a community, help artists increase visibility, and form international connections.

We are based in Poland, but we work with Belarusian photographers around the world.

Key projects

Podcast

Daliagliad (Далягляд):

 

Available on major platforms:

SpotifyApple PodcastsYouTube

Daliagliad is a podcast about Belarusian photography. In each episode, Katerina Kouzmitcheva and Yauhen Attsetski talk with guests about photography, creativity, and life. Their guests are photographers, curators, art historians, and other figures in the field of Belarusian photography.

Podcast

In One Shot (Адным кадрам):

In every episode, artist Yuliya Ruzhechka and her guest unpack one photograph by a Belarusian author, or, occasionally, a foreign author significant to the Belarusian context. They present its visual description to listeners, as well as the context of its creation and distribution of the photograph.

Artist talks with Belarusian authors

These are public meetings with Belarusian photographers, where authors present their projects, share their artistic experiences, and talk about their artistic approaches.

Self-presentation

Self-presentation is a meeting format where an author has 10 minutes to present themselves and their photo work to the audience. It can be a photo series or a project, a personal experience expressed through photography, or even their doubts. The audience can ask questions and participate in the discussion.

Photo Walk

Photo Walk is a meetup for photography enthusiasts. We gather at a café, get to know each other, and then follow a prepared route while photographing the city in the evening. Afterwards, we share our photos in a group chat, and some will be published on the FSH1 page. The walk is suitable for both beginners and more experienced photographers. The main goal is practice, inspiration, and enjoying the process of shooting.
